## Wildlife-Friendly Lighting

Traditional bright security lights can disrupt nocturnal animals (bats, moths, hedgehogs). Solar options excel here:

- **Warm White / Dimmer Glows:** Antheia and similar use softer 2700K-3000K tones less attractive to insects and less stressful for wildlife.
- **Motion Sensors (PIR):** Only activate when needed, reducing constant light trespass. Gardeners' World notes ground-angled designs further protect dark skies.
- **Low-Level & Decorative:** String/festoon styles (Lights4fun) provide ambience without flooding areas, supporting natural behaviors.
