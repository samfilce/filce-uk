## 2. Home Assistant Pet Monitoring — Free, Powerful, Local

If you're comfortable with a small amount of setup, **Home Assistant** transforms basic cameras into a full pet monitoring system — completely free, completely private, no cloud subscription.

**What Home Assistant adds:**
- Motion alerts when the dog enters/leaves a zone
- Notifications if barking is detected (with an audio sensor)
- Automations: turn on heating before you get home, flash a light if the dog isn't spotted after 2 hours
- Full history — see when your dog moved, when they ate, when they slept

## 5. Raised Dog Bowls — Ergonomics for Eating

Raised/elevated dog bowls reduce neck strain during eating — particularly relevant for large breeds who spend a long time eating. Proper posture also reduces the risk of bloat, which is a serious concern for deep-chested breeds.

### Polegas Elevated Dog Bowl Set

The **Polegas** set is a well-built UK-available option with adjustable height and stainless steel bowls that are easy to clean.

**What to look for in raised bowls:**
- Height appropriate for your dog's shoulder level
- Stainless steel bowls (hygienic, durable)
- Stable, non-tip base
- Dishwasher safe

## 6. Dog Training Aids — The Tech-Adjacent Gear That Works

Training technology ranges from clickers and spray deterrents to full electronic collar systems. For most dogs, the simplest tools are the most effective.

### Pet Corrector Spray — The Humane Interrupter

The **Pet Corrector** is a compressed air spray that produces a hiss to interrupt unwanted behaviour — jumping, barking, counter-surfing. It's the same sound as a predator (birds, snakes), which triggers a natural freeze response in dogs.

**It's not punishment.** It's an interrupter. Used correctly, it marks the moment a behaviour should stop and creates space to redirect to the right behaviour. The dog associates the hiss with the behaviour stopping, not with fear.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best camera for monitoring a dog at home UK?

The **Reolink Fisheye PoE** is the best all-round dog monitoring camera for UK homes. Its 180° lens covers an entire room with one camera, PoE power means zero WiFi dropouts, and it integrates natively with Home Assistant for smart alerts. It costs around £150 from Amazon UK.

For budget options, any 1080p WiFi camera with two-way audio will work — just expect occasional connectivity issues.

### Is Home Assistant free for pet monitoring?

Yes. Home Assistant is completely free and open-source. You pay for the hardware to run it on (a Raspberry Pi 4 costs around £50–70) but there are no subscription fees. All data is local — nothing goes to a cloud server.

### Are raised dog bowls really better?

For large breeds (Labs, Retrievers, Malamutes, German Shepherds), raised bowls reduce neck and shoulder strain during eating. They also help with dogs who push bowls around the floor. The evidence on preventing bloat is mixed, but the ergonomic benefits are genuine.

### What's the best dog bed for a large breed UK?

An orthopedic memory foam bed with a removable, washable cover. The **KSIIA XL** is a good option for dogs over 30kg. Look for a bolster design (raised edges) if your dog likes to lean when sleeping — most large breeds do.

### Can I monitor my dog remotely from work?

Yes — with a camera and a decent internet connection at home. A wired PoE camera running through a Home Assistant setup gives you live view, motion alerts, and two-way audio from anywhere in the world via the Home Assistant app (free, with a simple remote access configuration).

### How do I stop my dog barking when I'm away?

First, identify whether it's boredom, separation anxiety, or reactive barking (responding to sounds/people outside). A camera with audio helps you diagnose remotely. Solutions range from enrichment toys and exercise increases, to anti-anxiety products, to working with a behaviourist for serious cases. The **Pet Corrector** helps interrupt unwanted behaviour when you're present; for alone-time barking, remote monitoring and enrichment are more effective long-term.
